THE SILENT FAILURES

What a backup fails at, silently

  • Media nobody has read back

    The job succeeded. The bytes never did.

  • A schema left out of the dump

    Rows come back. The database does not.

  • A dependency that is down too

    The restore needs what the outage took.

  • Credentials nobody on shift has

    The path exists. Nobody can walk it.

None of these announce themselves. All of them wait.

No RPO or RTO figures are asserted in this deck — those numbers are yours to set and to measure.
