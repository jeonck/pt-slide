Platform engineering · Observability budget

Volume grows with traffic. Readership does not.

What multiplies

  • Requests per second
  • ×Log lines emitted per request
  • ×Replicas emitting the same line
  • ×Days retained, indexed, replicated

What does not

  • Incidents, one at a time
  • Engineers who open logs at all
  • The window they read: minutes
  • The three queries they know

Cost is a product. Readership is a constant. That gap is the whole argument.
