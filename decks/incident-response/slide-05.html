// 05 — SEVERITY

[ 05 / 06 ]

What each severity level obliges

LEVEL

WHEN IT APPLIES

WHAT IT OBLIGES

WHO IT WAKES

SEV-1

Customers blocked. No workaround exists.

Declare first, diagnose second.

On-call, service owner, duty exec.

SEV-2

Degraded. A workaround exists and it costs someone.

Commander named. Updates on cadence.

On-call and service owner.

SEV-3

Contained fault. No customer impact yet.

One owner. No separate comms role.

On-call only.

SEV-4

Known and tolerated. Tracked, not chased.

A ticket, not a page.

Nobody, until working hours.

A LEVEL IS A PERMISSION TO WAKE PEOPLE
