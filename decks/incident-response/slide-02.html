[ 02 / 06 ]

Three failures, none of them technical

[ FAILURE 01 ]

Everyone debugs.
Nobody speaks.

The interesting problem pulls in every pair of hands. Stakeholders go quiet, then arrive asking — and the asking slows the fix.

[ FAILURE 02 ]

Nobody says who
is in charge.

With no declared owner, every call needs consensus. Two people restart the same service; nobody owns the rollback.

[ FAILURE 03 ]

The timeline is
reconstructed.

Nobody writes while it happens. The review then argues about the order of events, from memory, instead of the cause.

Not one of these is a diagnosis problem. Each is a decision nobody made.
