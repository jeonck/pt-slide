Section 04 · The read

A record nobody reads is not a record

A named reader

“Someone reviews it”
means no one does

A review that belongs to everyone is always about to happen.

A fixed cadence

On a schedule,
or never at all

Read weekly, the record is a control. Read after the fact, it is only evidence.

A consequence

The read must
change something

If it cannot revoke a role, narrow a scope or close a gap, it is theatre.

A log with no reader or consequence audits as compliant and controls nothing.
