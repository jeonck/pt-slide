Section 02 · The price

What least privilege actually costs

Latency

The fix waits
on a question

The one who can repair it must ask first, and asking costs most at 03:00.

Attention

Someone has to
be awake to say yes

A permission that expires is requested again, and every request needs someone awake.

The missing map

You have to know
what is needed

Knowing what is needed means knowing it per service and per role, and it goes stale.

Least privilege priced honestly is not free — which is why standing access wins.
