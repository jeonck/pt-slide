## Checks
- [x] System consistency: PASS — One furniture system on all six sheets: an 11pt uppercase rail (context left, section right), a 24pt Source Serif 4 heading, a 0.5pt gold hairline divider, `main`, and a two-part footline (source caption left, disclaimer + pagination right), inside identical 40pt/32pt margins on a 640pt measure. Header and footer are **siblings** of `main` with `main{flex:1;min-height:0}`, so the gold divider and the footline sit at the same y on every content sheet — verified by flipping between slide-02.png, slide-04.png and slide-05.png, where the divider and both footline baselines do not move by a pixel. Sheets 01 and 06 drop the heading + divider (they are cover and closing) but keep the same rail, margins and footline y. Exactly **two backgrounds** (`#0A1A33` canvas, `#1F3A5F` panels), exactly **two typefaces** (Source Serif 4 for display, headings and claims; Inter for body, labels and footline), **one accent** (`#C8A24B`). Two content modules, each reused rather than reinvented: the ruled ledger row (02, and again for the prompts on 06) and the gold-hairline navy panel (03 as a triptych, 04 as a 2×2, 05 as a triptych with chevrons). No sheet invents a third.
- [x] Color discipline: PASS — The hex audit returns exactly six values: `#0A1A33`, `#1F3A5F`, `#3A5A85`, `#9AA6BC`, `#C8A24B`, `#E8E5DE`. Every one is a `ppt-goldman-ir-deck` spec token, verbatim. **No harmonic extension was needed and none was invented**, so there is no untracked colour to be Critical about. The computed ratios governed use rather than decorated it: `#3A5A85` is 2.47:1 on the canvas and is therefore confined to 1px row rules and never carries text; `#9AA6BC` is 7.08:1 on the canvas and 4.68:1 on a panel, so panel prose uses `#E8E5DE` (9.13:1) and `#9AA6BC` only appears on panels at 14pt where it clears 4.5; `#C8A24B` clears 4.5 on both grounds (7.22 / 4.77) and so is legitimate for the 11–12pt uppercase labels. Gold is never a fill — the Avoid list's first line — only 0.5pt hairlines, uppercase labels, step numbers, and one emphasised word on 01 and 06. `#1F3A5F` is only 1.51:1 against the canvas, which is exactly why every panel carries the spec's gold hairline border: without it the panel would not be visible as a panel.
