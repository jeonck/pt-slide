Section 03 · The record

What the elevation record has to capture

Who

A named human, not a role

A record that names a role names nobody. There is no one left to ask.

What

The account, role and resource

“Production” is a place, not a scope, and proves only that something happened.

Why

The ticket, written first

A reason given afterwards is a reason invented afterwards.

When it lapsed

The expiry, and who ended it

A grant with no observed end is standing access with a form attached.
