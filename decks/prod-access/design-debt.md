# design-debt — decks/prod-access

Major, Minor and Note findings accepted at the design gate rather than fixed. Each is a
deliberate trade, not an oversight.

| # | Sheet | What | Why it was accepted | What would resolve it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | all | The footline disclaimer is **10pt Inter**, where `ppt-goldman-ir-deck` specifies `disclaimer: Inter 8pt` | 8pt is below this framework's absolute 10pt floor, which is a Critical at the gate — the floor exists because unreadable text is unreadable regardless of what a spec says. The IR identity the spec is actually protecting (a disclaimer + pagination footline pinned bottom-right on every sheet, never omitted) is fully preserved; only the point size moved, and 10pt is the smallest value the framework allows | Nothing. The two rules are in direct conflict and the floor wins. If the canvas were the spec's 13.33in rather than 10in, the spec's 8pt would scale to 10.67pt and there would be no conflict at all |
| 2 | all | The spec's diagram vocabulary calls for a **monospace** face for step numbers and date labels; no monospace appears in the deck | The style's own Typography block declares exactly two faces — Source Serif 4 and Inter — and Pass A's system-consistency check caps a deck at two typefaces. Adding a third face to set six two-digit numbers would trade a system rule for a texture. The step numbers are Inter 600 12pt with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` and +0.06em tracking in gold, which keeps the tabular, wide-tracked reading the spec is after | Adding JetBrains Mono or similar as a third embedded face, and accepting the Pass A finding that goes with it |
| 3 | slide-05 | The gold chevrons between the three panels are small (9 × 14px, 1.2px stroke) and read as faint at presentation distance | The Avoid list bans icons and clipart, and the spec asks for "restrained chevron arrowheads" specifically. A chevron large enough to be conspicuous stops being a connector and starts being an icon. The panel sequence is also carried by the kickers themselves (reader → cadence → consequence), so the chevrons confirm the reading rather than supply it | A 20pt gutter is the constraint; widening it to 32pt would allow a heavier chevron, at the cost of 24pt off the three panel measures, which would re-wrap every mechanism line |
| 4 | all | Body leading is 1.45 and serif claims 1.4, where the spec specifies body leading 1.4 | The framework floors body leading at 1.4 because tighter values clip ascenders and descenders — and it caught this deck twice: `validate` raised `text-clipped` on the 46pt cover display and the 34pt closing display at 1.3 | Nothing; the floor wins |
| 5 | slide-02 | The last ledger row's 1px navy rule is set to `transparent` rather than removed | Removing the border outright would make the last row 0.75pt shorter than its three siblings — the "emphasise one row and only that row shifts" trap. Keeping the box and hiding the colour costs nothing and keeps every row identical | Nothing; this is the correct form of the fix |
| 6 | slide-04 | The four record fields are a 2 × 2 grid, where slides 03 and 05 use the same panel as a horizontal triptych | Four fields do not fit a triptych, and splitting them 3 + 1 would imply a hierarchy the schema does not have. It is the same module in a different arrangement, not a third pattern | Nothing |
| 7 | all | Panel fill `#1F3A5F` is only **1.51:1** against the canvas `#0A1A33`, so panels are legible as panels only because of their gold hairline | Both are spec tokens and the spec pairs them deliberately; the hairline is the style's stated mechanism for bounding a panel ("절대 면으로 쓰지 않는다"). Substituting a lighter fill would mean inventing a colour, which is Critical | Nothing available inside the palette |
