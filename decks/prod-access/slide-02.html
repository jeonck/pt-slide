Why permanent access accumulates

#

The grant

What makes it permanent

01

Never revoked

Granted for an incident, and the incident ended

The role outlives the reason. Nothing knows the reason ended.

02

Inherited

Granted to a group, and the group kept growing

Access follows the team, not the task. Joining inherits all of it.

03

The exception

Granted to unblock a launch, and the launch shipped

Removing it costs a conversation. Keeping it costs nothing.

Access is the only thing we add without a review date.
