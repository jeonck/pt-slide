## Checks
- [x] Composition & hierarchy: PASS — One job per sheet, one anchor per sheet. slide-01.png: the anchor is two full-measure lines of 46pt Source Serif 4, the second in the deck's only gold — a real typographic anchor, not a title in a box — with the answer already visible in the hairlined panel beside the premise. slide-02.png: a bounded financial table, gold rule above and below, closing on a gold-ruled total row that states the mechanism in one line. slide-03.png: three equal panels under one question, closed by a gold-ruled note that turns the sheet ("which is why standing access wins"). slide-04.png: the schema sheet — four panels, four field names in gold, the requirement in serif and the failure mode underneath; this is the `iac-drift` one-liner given a whole sheet. slide-05.png: the same panel module in sequence with gold chevrons, so the three conditions read as a chain that has to close rather than a list. slide-06.png: the closing anchor is "What we / need to / decide" at 34pt with `decide` in gold, balanced against a gold-ruled ledger of three questions. **Both the cover and the closing sheet carry a genuine visual anchor.** Checked specifically on the contact sheet: the gold divider is at an identical y on 02–05 and the footline baseline is identical on all six.
- [x] Typography & legibility: PASS — One scale across the deck, verified by extracting every `font-size`: 46 / 34 / 24 / 16 / 15 / 14 / 12 / 11 / 10pt and nothing else. **Minimum is 10pt** (the footline, exactly at the floor and nowhere below it) and no body copy is below 14pt. `line-height: 1` appears nowhere; body leading is 1.45, serif claims 1.4, display 1.35. Two `text-clipped` errors at 1.3 on the 46pt cover and 34pt closing display were caught by `validate` and fixed by raising leading, not by shrinking type. Contrast was computed, not eyeballed: text 13.81:1 on the canvas and 9.13:1 on a panel; gold 7.22 / 4.77; muted 7.08 / 4.68. `#3A5A85` measured 2.47:1 on the canvas and was therefore removed from text duty entirely — it is only 1px row rules. **The presenter line was checked by name**, because that is the line that goes invisible on dark grounds: `PRESENTER · TEAM` is `#9AA6BC` at 7.08:1 and is plainly legible in both slide-01.png and slide-06.png. The panels are only 1.51:1 against the canvas, so the gold hairline is what makes them read as panels — confirmed on slide-03.png and slide-04.png, where every panel edge is unambiguous.
- [x] Korean/CJK word-break integrity: PASS (not applicable) — this is an English deck; there is no Hangul or CJK and the four Pretendard faces the scaffolder installs were deleted. English wrapping was inspected in their place and the equivalent faults were found and fixed on the renders: the source caption wrapped to two lines on **all six sheets** and pushed content down under itself; two of three mechanism lines on slide-02 wrapped and dropped the gold total row onto the footline; the WHAT panel's serif requirement on slide-04 wrapped and burst its panel; and three panels ended on one-word runt lines ("most.", "awake.", "does."), all rewritten. In the current render **no line anywhere ends in an orphan word, no heading wraps, and no footline runs to two lines** — checked sheet by sheet at full size.
- [x] Review Litmus: PASS — Three to five seconds per sheet. slide-02.png reads as "three ordinary grants, none of which ever ended" from the ledger structure before a sentence is read. slide-04.png reads as WHO / WHAT / WHY / WHEN IT LAPSED — the record's four fields — from the gold labels alone, which is the point of the sheet. slide-06.png reads as three numbered decisions. Stripping decoration is not available: there is no decoration. The visual vocabulary is serif type, navy panels and gold hairlines, exactly what the spec's Avoid list demands. On removable lines: eleven were in fact removed during the render passes — the slide-03 note lost a clause, slide-05's note lost four words, slide-04's WHY panel lost thirteen — and every one of those sentences got better, which is the usual sign the line was removable.
