# design-debt — decks/alert-design

Notes accepted at the design gate rather than fixed. Nothing here blocks; each is a place where
the deck and the `ppt-monochrome-risk` spec knowingly differ, with the reason.

| # | Where | Debt | Why it was accepted |
|---|-------|------|---------------------|
| 1 | deck-wide | **Arimo, not Helvetica Neue.** The spec names Helvetica Neue with Arial Black / Arial fallbacks. | None of the three is distributable or available on npm, and `slides-grab` forbids remote font URLs in saved slide HTML. Arimo is metric-compatible with Helvetica and Arial; the same substitution was made in `decks/mlops-platform`. Arimo has no Black weight, so the 40pt cover headline is Arimo 700 — the spec's hard requirement is "Bold or heavier", which 700 meets. If a licensed Helvetica Neue ever lands in `assets/fonts/`, swapping the two `@font-face` rules is the whole change; the character budgets were computed with the 0.48 coefficient, which was measured on Arimo in this repo, so they would need re-measuring. |
| 2 | deck-wide | **Type sizes are not the spec's absolute points.** | The spec targets a 13.33in slide; this canvas is 10in wide, a 0.75x scale. Its 17pt body, 12pt label and 10pt caption become 12.75pt, 9pt and 7.5pt — all below this repo's 14pt body and 10pt absolute floors, and under-10pt text is a Critical at the gate. Type is therefore held at 14pt body / 10–12pt labels / 22–24pt subheads / 40pt display, and the spec's *ratios* are preserved rather than its absolute values. |
| 3 | slide-03 | **`diagram.render` says "SVG precise, no div blocks"; the gate nodes are HTML boxes.** | This repo requires slide text to live in semantic tags so the PPTX text engine and screen readers can reach it, and text baked into SVG is neither editable nor searchable. The nodes are drawn to the spec's geometry — 0px radius, 2px `#0A0A0A` border, 22pt black square number chip with a white digit — and only the right-angle connectors with their sharp triangle heads are inline SVG. The rendered result matches what the spec describes; the mechanism differs. |
| 4 | slide-03 | **Three process-flow steps plus a terminal bar, where the spec asks for 4–5 horizontal steps.** | The argument has exactly three tests. A fourth would have to be invented, which is a worse failure than a short flow. The full-width black-fill bar under the row is the fourth stage — the outcome — and is the spec's own inversion device. |
| 5 | slide-03 | **`#E6E6E6` is used as a surface once**, for the "if no" strip inside each gate node. | Two background values total, `#FFFFFF` and `#E6E6E6`, which is the ceiling. Luminance de-emphasis is the spec's stated mechanism, and the strip's `#3D3D3D` text on `#E6E6E6` is ~8.2:1. |
| 6 | slide-02 | **The two 4px column dividers are standalone flex items with 8pt of white on each side**, not borders on the columns. | A `border-left` on the TICKET column sat flush against the black-filled PAGE block and was swallowed by it, so only one of the two dividers rendered. Giving the rules their own boxes means no neighbouring fill can absorb them, and both columns keep identical padding so nothing shifts. |
| 7 | slide-01, slide-05 | **`PRESENTER · TEAM` is a placeholder.** | No name was invented. Replace in `slide-01.html` (the footer `<p>`) and `slide-05.html` (the footer caption) before presenting. |
