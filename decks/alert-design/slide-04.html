04 · DISPOSITION

What to do with the alerts that fail

A failing alert is not left alone. It is deleted, downgraded, or fixed.

A

DELETE

It has never once changed what anyone did. Deleting it removes noise and nothing else; the check that produced it can stay.

B

DOWNGRADE

The condition is real and it can wait. Move it to a queue with a named owner and a date. A promise is not the same as silence.

C

FIX

It fires because the system needs a human to survive. Repair the system and the alert deletes itself. Only this one lowers the count.
