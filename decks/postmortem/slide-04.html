What the room is deciding

Every account is a bet

The person who knows what happened is also the person with the most to lose by saying it. They are estimating what the transcript gets used for later, and the estimate is usually right. Change the estimate, not the tone.

Four conditions, none of them tone

  • It is not evidence. Nothing said here reaches a performance file or a promotion packet.

  • Not knowing is sayable. “I did not know that flag existed” has to cost nothing to say.

  • Timeline before conclusion. Order the facts first, or the room reverse-engineers a culprit.

  • The closest person reads it. Whoever was at the keyboard writes it, or signs it before filing.

Source — none. Every claim here is mechanical, not measured.
