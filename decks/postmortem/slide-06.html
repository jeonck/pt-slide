What we need to decide

Decision one

Who writes it

The person at the keyboard, the service owner, or an author who was not on the incident? Name the default, and name who may override it.

Decision two

What the deadline is

How many working days from resolution to a filed review? And who is allowed to say it slips, to whom, and on what record?

Decision three

When items are not done

What happens on the date? “Nothing” is a permitted answer. It only has to be the answer we chose out loud, before the date.

Blameless costs three concessions. We pay them, or we stop using the word.
