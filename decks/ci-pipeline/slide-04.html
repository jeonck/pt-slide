POLICY

Two policies that keep the signal honest

WHY BOTH EXIST

The gate’s only asset is that a red build means something.

Both policies protect that one asset. Neither of them is really about the test.

01

A flaky test is a broken test.

A test that fails without a cause teaches the team to read failures as noise. Quarantine it the day it flakes, then fix it or delete it — a retry is not a fix.

02

A pipeline nobody trusts gets bypassed.

Not by rebellion — by batching commits, merging on “probably green”, and handing out overrides. The gate holds only what people will actually wait for.
