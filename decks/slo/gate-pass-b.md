English wrapping was inspected in its place, against measured advance widths rather than by eye, and the equivalent faults were found and fixed: two right-panel items on slide 02 wrapped to a second line while their left-panel counterparts did not, and the slide-06 closing statement overran its measure by 8.3pt and stranded "anything." on a line of its own.
