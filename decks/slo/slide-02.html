02 · THE SWITCH

SHEET 02 / 06

An SLO is not a quality metric. It is a switch.

READ AS A METRIC

  • It describes how the service behaved.

  • It gets reported, compared, discussed.

  • Nothing about next week has to change.

USED AS A SWITCH

  • It has two positions and only two.

  • Crossing it changes what ships next.

  • No meeting is needed for it to fire.

A target that has never stopped anything is a dashboard with a threshold drawn on it.
