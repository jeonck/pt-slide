Three ways an SLO stops being a switch

The number stays exactly where it was. What goes missing is the rule attached to it.

01

A target nobody chose

It came off a vendor page. Nobody in the room would defend it, so nobody enforces it either.

02

A budget with no teeth

The chart turns red, the sprint carries on, and the budget quietly becomes a colour.

03

An exception each time

Every breach arrives with a good reason. A switch that bends has only one position.

Each of these is a decision nobody wrote down, showing up later as a habit.
