Four rules that keep the ledger trustworthy

Fig. 04 / 05

RULE

WHAT IT SAYS

WHAT BREAKS WITHOUT IT

R-01

One name, one lineage

A name never changes meaning.

Two teams ship different things under one name.

R-02

Deprecate before delete

Archived records stay readable.

An audit cannot reconstruct last quarter.

R-03

Production names its rollback

The previous version stays promotable.

Rollback becomes a rebuild.

R-04

Stage changes append

The ledger is never edited in place.

Nobody can say who promoted what.
