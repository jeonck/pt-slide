Four questions separate the styles you will meet

01

Deployment unit

What has to ship together for a change to take effect?

02

Data ownership

Who may write this table, and who may read it directly?

03

Call direction

Does the caller wait for the answer, or learn about it later?

04

Failure radius

When this part stops, what else stops with it?

Answer all four and the style is already chosen; answer none and the name is a guess.
